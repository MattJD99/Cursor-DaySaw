2.  **Information Collected Automatically:**
    * **Log and Usage Data:** When you access our website or Services, our servers may automatically record information, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and dates/times of access.
    * **Cookies and Tracking Technologies:** We may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your interaction with our website and Services, to personalize your experience, and for analytics. You can control the use of cookies at the individual browser level.

3.  **Information from Third Parties:**
    * We may receive information about you from other sources, such as public databases, marketing partners, or social media platforms, if you interact with our brand on those platforms.
